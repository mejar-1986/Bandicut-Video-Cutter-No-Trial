# Bandicut Video Cutter

## Overview

Bandicut Video Cutter is a software tool designed for Windows users to edit video files by cutting, splitting, and joining video segments. It focuses on providing a straightforward way to trim videos without re-encoding, maintaining the original video quality and reducing processing time.

## Key Features

**Video cutting without quality loss**  
**Fast processing with minimal CPU usage**  
**Support for various video formats including MP4, AVI, MOV, and more**  
**Simple interface for precise segment selection**  
**Ability to join multiple video clips into a single file**  
**Frame-by-frame video preview for accurate editing**  
**Option to extract audio from video files**  

## Why Bandicut Video Cutter?

Bandicut Video Cutter is chosen for its clear and user-friendly approach to video editing on Windows platforms. Using Bandicut Video Cutter allows users to save time by avoiding full video re-encoding, preserving video quality throughout the editing process. Its efficient handling of video files ensures smoother workflow and reduces system resource load during video cutting tasks.
